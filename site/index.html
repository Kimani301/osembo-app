How to install

  1. Open this page on your Android phone and tap the download button.
  2. When the download finishes, tap the file (or open Downloads and tap Osembo.apk).
  3. If Android asks, tap Settings and allow Install unknown apps for your browser, then go back and tap Install.
  4. Open Osembo and log in with the details your school admin gave you.

Updating

Download the new file the same way and install it over the old one. Do not uninstall first, or you will lose the data saved on your phone.

Need help?

Contact your school admin. This app works on Android phones only. iPhones cannot install it.
